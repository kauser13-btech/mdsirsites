Dadasaheb Phalke Excellence Awards 2017

Bashundhara Group Managing Director Sayem Sobhan Anvir has received India’s most prestigious Dadasaheb Phalke Excellence Awards-2017 on Friday. He has been accorded the award for the outstanding contribution of East West Media Group Ltd, a concern of the country’s leading business conglomerate Bashundhara Group, to mass media and social service. Sayem Sobhan is the Managing Director also of East West Media Group Ltd.
